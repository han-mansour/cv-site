02
Opportunity discovery

Why, and what?

GoalUnderstand the problem space, find opportunities to solve as a team, and prioritise the most impactful ones.

Why now?

~$3M ARR up for renewal

20 Tier 1 accounts with ~4.5K users were at risk, with renewals due in the next 8 months.

What's the user problem?

Low usage, missing features

  • Weekly active usage in these accounts was far lower than other Enterprise orgs.
  • User feedback and win/loss analysis pointed to missing features.
  • Research with ~30 customers on workflows, JTBD and opportunities.
What are the alternatives?

Competitor benchmarking

Compared competitor tools' plan-page features and pricing, and analysed their UX.

I then ran a cross-functional workshop with engineering, design, data, CS and marketing to share the findings, get everyone aligned and gather opportunities together.

An iterative path to market

Once we understood the flows, we could plan a continuous, staged route to launch.

Phase 1

Alpha testing

2 months

A minimum viable product to get the 20 target Enterprise orgs using it, so we could monitor performance and gather feedback.

Phase 2

Beta testing

3 months

A minimum lovable product for all Enterprise customers, to gather feedback and improve more of the user journey.

Phase 3

Launch & GTM

Launched to all Enterprise and Self Serve users with a fully end-to-end tested solution.

What we considered along the way
Technical
  • Understand customer technologies
  • Learn to build an Electron app and overcome blockers
  • Track transcription services by type
  • Successful vs failed transcriptions
  • Manual vs automated deployment needs
UX
  • Usability testing with prototypes
  • User story and flow mapping into key journey steps and edge cases
  • In-app "ease of use" surveys
  • Behavioural data on feature usage
Commercial
  • Onboarding materials per user type
  • Training Sales and CS on positioning and onboarding
  • Support articles and how-tos
  • Feature naming and positioning
  • Launch strategy
  • Pricing and packaging
  • Revenue attribution reporting
Operational
  • Sequencing around other teams' dependencies

Lessons learned
What was hard

Split-focus work streams are overwhelming

Next time
  • Reduce product team scope where possible.
  • Have separate groups of people working on different problem areas.
What was hard

Keep on top of initiatives that could affect your team's area

Next time
  • Stay in sync with teams working on similar areas by joining their planning sessions.
  • Better updates between EMs, PMs and PDs on short-term focus.
